Help test Palomares

Record the ride, then look back at the corners: your lean, speed and how the bike slowed and accelerated. Replay it on the map and explore the report afterward.

About invitations

iPhone with iOS 26+ · a small invited group · free during the beta

What it does

Your phone’s sensors, on your bike, analyzed privately on your phone.

See how one corner led into the next, revisit the moments worth a look, and compare a familiar stretch with your own earlier rides. A real ride on Palomares Road comes with the app, so you can explore before recording your own.

Screens from the current Palomares Road sample that comes with the app.

The beta

Try it on your own bike, and tell me where it’s right and where it’s wrong.

What you need

  • An iPhone running iOS 26+.
  • A secure motorcycle phone mount that keeps the phone stable. Follow the mount maker’s instructions for your phone and motorcycle.
  • Apple’s TestFlight app and an invitation when the beta is ready.
  • A few rides of 20 minutes or more over the next few weeks.

Ride safely

  • Mount the phone and start recording while parked. Keep your attention on the road, and park before using the app’s on-screen controls or reviewing a ride.
  • Lean and other numbers are estimates from the phone’s sensors. They can be wrong, and finding out where is part of the test.
  • This is a test version. It may crash or lose a ride.
  • Ride your ride and learn a bit more about how you do it!

What I’d like you to do

Five steps

  1. Set up before you ride. Add your motorcycle and mount, allow location and motion, and start the recording before you set off.
  2. Ride normally, with the screen locked if that’s what you usually do. The app will also work just fine in the background if you use navigation or something else. Stop the recording when you’re parked.
  3. Review your ride afterward: the replay, the corners worth a look and the report. Does it match how the ride felt?
  4. Optionally send a ride for review. In the ride’s ⋯ menu, choose Send for review, check what the file contains in the privacy policy, and email it to beta@palomares.app.
  5. Tell me how it went. After two or three rides, email what worked, what felt wrong, and anything that was confusing. TestFlight feedback is useful for bugs and screenshots.

Feedback and ride submissions are optional. You can use the beta without sending a ride. Check screenshots and attachments before sending them: they may include places, notes or other details you want to keep private.

Your rides and your privacy

Your rides stay yours.

✓
On your phone and your iCloud Drive
No Palomares account, ads or in-app analytics. Analysis runs on your device; an optional iCloud Drive folder lets your devices share rides.
✓
Choose what you share
Ride pictures and Reels show the route without a map. Flyovers and replay clips show the map. Trimming the start and finish is optional for whole-ride pictures, Reels and Flyovers.
✓
Sent only when you choose
Send for review always cuts back the start and finish and leaves out raw sensor recordings. The remaining route, notes and answers can still reveal personal details.

The details are in the privacy policy.

Feedback

Email beta@palomares.app with your phone and iOS version, bike and mount, whether recording worked, and whether the replay and report matched how the ride felt.

For a bug, include what you were doing and what you expected. You can also send a screenshot or crash feedback through TestFlight. Send a review ZIP only if you want help looking at a particular ride.

Email is handled through Zoho Mail. Read how feedback and attachments are handled.
